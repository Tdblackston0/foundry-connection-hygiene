## 1. Principles

1. No inspection, adjustment, or repair of powered equipment takes place until the equipment is isolated and verified de-energised under the site Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) procedure SOP-SAF-003.
2. Guards, interlocks, light curtains, and pull-cords are never bypassed, bridged, or defeated for production reasons. Temporary bypass requires a written risk assessment approved by the Site Engineering Manager and the HSE Lead, with a defined duration and compensating controls.
3. Only personnel holding the relevant site authorisation may perform mechanical or electrical work. Authorisation levels are held in the training matrix.
4. Equipment that has tripped on a protective device (overtemperature, overcurrent, interlock) is not reset for a second attempt without a documented reason. A single reset by the operator is permitted; a second trip requires maintenance involvement before any further reset.

## 2. Who may do what

| Activity | Minimum authorisation |
|---|---|
| Visual inspection from outside guarding, equipment running | Operator |
| Handheld temperature or vibration reading from outside guarding | Operator with PdM awareness training |
| Opening guards or enclosures | Authorised Maintenance Technician, after LOTO |
| Electrical measurement in live panels | Authorised Electrician, under SOP-ELE-004 |
| Entering a conveyor or press envelope | Authorised technician under LOTO plus permit-to-work |
| Restart after protective trip (second or later) | Maintenance Supervisor approval recorded in CMMS |

## 3. Reporting

- All unplanned stops over 15 minutes are logged as incidents in the CMMS within the shift.
- Observations of abnormal noise, heat, smell, or tracking are logged even if production continues.
